I. Note-Taking Fill-Ins

  • Empirical Probability: Based on observed data / relative frequency.
  • Classical Probability: Based on theoretical outcomes / sample space.
  • General Addition Rule: If events are disjoint, \( P(E \text{ and } F) \) is zero (0).
  • Complement Rule: Use when you see "not" or "at least one".
  • Conditional Probability: The symbol "|" means "given that".

II. Problem Set Solutions

Solution 1 (General Addition Rule):

\( P(S \text{ or } B) = P(S) + P(B) - P(S \text{ and } B) \)

\( P(S \text{ or } B) = \frac{18}{30} + \frac{12}{30} - \frac{5}{30} = \frac{25}{30} \)

Answer: \( \frac{5}{6} \) or \( \approx 0.833 \)

Solution 2 (Independence):

\( P(4 \text{ and } T) = P(4) \cdot P(T) \)

\( P(4 \text{ and } T) = \frac{1}{6} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \)

Answer: \( \frac{1}{12} \) or \( \approx 0.083 \)

Solution 3 (Conditional):

\( P(F|D) = \frac{P(D \text{ and } F)}{P(D)} \)

\( P(F|D) = \frac{0.30}{0.45} = \frac{2}{3} \)

Answer: \( \approx 0.667 \)

Solution 4 (General Multiplication):

Note: Without replacement changes the denominator.

\( P(B_1 \text{ and } R_2) = P(B_1) \cdot P(R_2|B_1) \)

\( P(B_1 \text{ and } R_2) = \frac{7}{10} \cdot \frac{3}{9} = \frac{21}{90} \)

Answer: \( \frac{7}{30} \) or \( \approx 0.233 \)
