STAT 2050 Project: The Class Profile

Instructor & Student Guide

Project Overview & Learning Outcomes

Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) Assessed:

  • CLO 1: Analyze data with descriptive and numerical statistics (visualizations, measures of central tendency, and variance).
  • CLO 2: Calculate probabilities for discrete and continuous distributions with an understanding of probability concepts and randomness.
  • CLO 3: Analyze data using regression and correlation techniques.
  • CLO 4: Construct hypothesis tests and confidence intervals for population parameters.
  • CLO 5: Construct clear, data-driven statistical arguments from results.

General Instructions:

  • Data Source: See individual phase instructions for which dataset file to use.
  • Submission Format: For written components, you may create your project however you like (Word, Google Docs, etc.), but the final document must be exported and uploaded as a PDF to ensure formatting is preserved. Phase 1 requires two separate submissions: a Google Sheets link and a PDF document.
  • Mathematical Notation: If you struggle to type mathematical symbols or formulas digitally, you may neatly hand-write your equations, take a clear photo, and insert the image directly into your final document.
Academic Integrity & AI Policy:
This project must be your own original work. The use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools (such as ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) to generate text, analysis, or calculations is strictly prohibited and will be treated as academic dishonesty.

Exception: You may use tools like Grammarly solely for spell-checking and sentence structure improvements. You are also permitted to use AI exclusively for the designated Phase 1 Career Exploration prompt. All Data Analysis must be your own work.

Phase 1: Variable Identification, Data Cleaning & AI Exploration

Focus: Classifying data types, ensuring data integrity, and exploring the real-world utility of statistics.

Student Instructions

Step 1: Setup and Scenario

The Scenario: Your boss has given you this raw class survey data to clean up before the rest of the team can analyze it. You need to review the variables, determine what reasonable values are, and define if the variables are qualitative or quantitative.

Open a blank Google Sheet. You will need to set up three separate tabs (sheets) at the bottom of the document to present your work to your boss:

  1. Variable Analysis
  2. Documented Changes
  3. Cleaned Data

(You can view an example of how your final spreadsheet should be organized here: Example Phase 1 Spreadsheet).

Step 2: Variable Analysis (Tab 1)

Review the questions asked in the raw class survey. In your Variable Analysis tab, create a table listing each variable collected (e.g., Height, Pulse, Eye Color, Math Anxiety, etc.), and provide the following classifications for each:

  • Is the variable Qualitative (Categorical) or Quantitative (Numerical)?
  • What is the level of measurement: Nominal, Ordinal, Interval, or Ratio (NOIR)?

Step 3: Data Cleaning & Documentation (Tabs 2 & 3)

Real-world data is rarely perfect! Paste the raw class survey data into your Cleaned Data tab. Review the data carefully and clean it up based on the following guidelines:

  • Quantitative Variables: Any quantitative variables should have numeric values of the same units. For example, if you find something is "6ft2in", and it is in a variable that should be quantitative with a unit of inches, you will need to convert it to inches and remove the label.
  • Categorical Variables: If you have a categorical variable, but the values are not entered consistently (like "blue" and "Blue", or "green" and "Green"), you will want to choose a particular capitalization and make the data consistent.

Crucial Requirement: Since this is for your boss, any change you make needs to be documented in your Documented Changes tab so you can later explain how you approached the project, and why you made the changes you made. Keep a clear log of the variable, the original issue, and how you fixed it.

Submission Note: You will submit the URL to this Google Sheet to the Phase 1 Data Assignment link. Ensure the link permissions are set to "Anyone with the link can view".

Step 4: AI Career Exploration

For this specific step, you are encouraged to use an AI assistant (like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude) to explore how statistics applies to your specific field of study, your personal life, and the world at large.

Think about a few hobbies, sports, health interests, or passions you have outside of school. Then, copy and paste the following prompt into the AI tool, making sure to fill in the bracketed information with your own details:

"I am a [Insert Your Major] major. I also enjoy [Insert 2-3 personal interests, e.g., rock climbing, playing video games, and budgeting my finances]. Explain why my degree program requires a statistics course, and explain where I might see statistics and probability in my daily life. Next, explain how AI (like yourself) uses probability to generate responses, and how the medical field uses inferential statistics to determine the effectiveness and side effects of new drugs. Finally, provide two real-world examples of how professionals in my field use inferential statistics to make decisions, and two examples of how I might use statistics or probability to better understand or improve my personal interests."

Reflection & Transparency Requirement: Read the AI's response carefully. Open a new document (Word, Google Docs, etc.) and complete the following:

  • Your Summary: Write a brief summary (4-5 sentences) in your own words explaining what you learned. Did anything surprise you about how statistics connects to your major, your hobbies, or broader topics like AI and medicine? Were there any ridiculous suggestions? If so, please share them in your summary. This portion must not be generated by AI.
  • AI Tool Used: Below your summary, explicitly state which AI assistant you used (e.g., "AI Tool Used: ChatGPT").
  • Chat Transcript: Paste the entire chat transcript below your summary, including your initial customized prompt and the AI's complete response.

Export this final document as a PDF to submit.
